02 · Zero-error experiment

Close the jaws completely. A perfect instrument should read zero.

0.0Zero reading (mm)
0.0Zero error (mm)
0.0Correction (mm)
Corrected reading = Observed reading − Zero error

The instrument has no zero error. The observed reading is the corrected reading.

4.6 mmCorrected object measurement
Positive zero error: subtract it. Negative zero error: subtracting a negative value means adding its magnitude.
